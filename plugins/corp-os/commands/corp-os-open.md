Invoke the `corp-os-decide` skill.

If the person named a specific fork, that is what to work on: `$ARGUMENTS`

With no arguments this is the standing review. Order by consequence, not by age: anything past its `decide_by` comes first, because that choice is being made by default right now and the record should say which option is winning by inaction. Then what is blocking an active job, then what has no owner or no date — those are the ones that will still be here next quarter, and fixing the field is usually one question.

End on the decision closest to its date and the one thing that would settle it.
