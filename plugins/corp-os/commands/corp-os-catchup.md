description: Pull what is new from your connected sources and see what moved
---

Invoke the `corp-os-pull` skill.

Scope, if the person gave one: `$ARGUMENTS`

With no arguments, default to everything since each source's recorded cutoff — that is what "catch me up" means and asking which sources first wastes the one thing the OS already knows. Do ask for a window if a source has no cutoff at all, since a first pull that dumps two years of history buries whatever mattered.

Lead the report with what actually moved a job or an open item, not with counts. "Twelve meetings pulled, two bear on the renewal" is the useful sentence; "twelve meetings pulled" is not.
