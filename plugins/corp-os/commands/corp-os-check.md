---

Invoke the `corp-os-reality-check` skill.

Scope, if given: `$ARGUMENTS`

With no arguments, sweep everything but work it in batches — a worklist of sixty presented at once gets abandoned, and ten resolved is a real improvement. Order by what a wrong answer would cost rather than by age: claims an active job depends on first, decisions and constraints ahead of facts, anything already published ahead of internal-only.

Keep the unsourced entries in their own bucket. They can never be re-confirmed, so carrying them as overdue produces a backlog nobody can clear, and a sweep that always ends in failure is a sweep people stop running.
