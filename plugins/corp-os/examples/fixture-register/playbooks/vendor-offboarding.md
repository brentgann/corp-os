Hand-written. Not derived from anything in `raw/`, and nothing in the corpus would reconstruct it.

1. Freeze the PO before the notice period starts, not after — the notice clock and the PO clock are not the same clock and assuming they are has cost us twice.
2. Pull the data export while the contract is still live. Access dies with the contract, not with the notice.
3. Get the deletion attestation in writing. Verbal confirmation is worth nothing at audit.
4. Keep the correspondence. It is the only record of what was promised at the end.
