Hand-written. Learned the hard way, never written down anywhere else.

- Account manager first, always, and in writing. It is slower and it is the only version that exists later.
- Their manager only after a missed commitment, never after a missed reply.
- Legal only once a contract term is actually in play. Reaching for it earlier costs the relationship and buys nothing.
